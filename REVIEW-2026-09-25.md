# Review 2026-09-25

This was a whole-monorepo review and refactoring run. It is based on `main` at 16b120a. It was delivered on `feature/review-2026-09-25` as 20 signed commits, with the head at 8db093d, followed by one commit that adds this record and the release notes. The branch changes 241 files. Every commit that ran the pre-commit hook passed lint, typecheck, the unit suites, the BDD suites and the integration suite. At the head, that is 3,425 unit, 2,822 BDD and 147 integration tests.

The precedents are `AUDIT.md` (2026-04-02) and `AUDIT-mimo-2026-06-14.md`. This record keeps the same shape. It covers what was reviewed, what was found, what was done about each item, and what is left.

## How it ran

There were five orchestrated workflows. Each was a fan-out of independent agents that used adversarial verification before acting on anything. Findings were deduplicated by file and line. Every finding was handed to at least one refuter, and anything rated major or above went to a second refuter at higher effort. Fixes ran in worktrees partitioned by file cluster so no two agents edited the same file. Each patch was applied, rebuilt and re-tested on the branch before it was committed.

## Outcome

- The two review passes confirmed 200 findings. There were 154 in the first pass over `main` and 46 in the cold review of the finished branch. Of these findings, 190 are fixed, 1 is partly fixed and 9 are skipped with a recorded reason. By severity, the breakdown is 1 critical, 39 major, 132 minor and 28 nits.
- There were 15 refactor candidates, and 14 were carried out. The removal of the deprecated core adapter-option types was deferred to the next major release.
- The one critical item, the hostname-only SSRF check on external `$ref`s, is closed. Every address a host resolves to is checked at connect time over a pinned connection. Redirects are re-checked per hop, and `maxBytes` is enforced while streaming.
- There is new core surface for adapter and plugin authors. It includes the `@schmock/core/adapter` subpath (`acquireRequestAdmission`, `awaitWithAbort`, `abortReason`, `createFetchInterceptor`) and the root helpers `serveNodeRequest`, `getResponseParts`, `replaceResponseBody`, `parsePathPrefix`, `matchPathPrefix`, `withDefaultContentType`, `buildFormattedErrorResponse`, `redactHeaders`, `getHeader` and `SENSITIVE_HEADER_NAMES`. The Express, Angular, CLI, openapi, validation and query packages consume them instead of their own copies.
- Three large files were split along their seams. These are `core/builder.ts` (route table, history, node server, admission), `cli/cli.ts` (args, seed manifest, admin, CORS, request, server, watch) and `openapi/parser.ts` (document loading, operation extraction).
- The 33 behaviour changes from the refactor waves and the wave-3 fixes are each pinned by a test and listed in `project/release-notes-2026-09-25.md`.

## Before release

- Bump the minor version of `@schmock/core` and raise the `peerDependencies` range in `@schmock/express`, `@schmock/angular`, `@schmock/vue`, `@schmock/validation` and `@schmock/query`, and the dependency range in `@schmock/openapi` (all still `^2.4.1`). They import exports that only the new core has.
- The seeded faker snapshots change against 2.4.1. Explicit keywords win over field-name heuristics, and weighted booleans draw from the seeded RNG. Regenerate them.
- Read `project/release-notes-2026-09-25.md` before writing the changelog. Every user-visible change on the branch is there, grouped by package.
